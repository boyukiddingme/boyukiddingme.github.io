01 About

I like projects where there is something real to understand — a machine, a material, a system, a user or a place.

I enjoy taking vague problems and turning them into something concrete: a test, prototype, workflow, interface, experiment or working system.

My interests move across disciplines, but the process is usually the same: observe carefully, find the actual constraint, build something, test it, then iterate.
